# Racing quotations in the Box

The best-lap, spin and impact pools each contain 50 positive messages in German and English.
Eleven variants refer to short racing quotations. German excerpts are our own translations;
the encouragement following them is the Box speaking. No driver endorsement is implied.
The Box does not claim its own racing history or damage that the telemetry cannot report.
